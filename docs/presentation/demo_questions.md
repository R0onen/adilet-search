## Demo tip

Earlier checks ranked the expected article first for questions 1–3 and equivalent formulations of questions 4–5. These exact formulations have not all been tested, especially questions 6–10. Open the source article to verify every answer, particularly exceptions. A valid citation does not guarantee that the explanation is correct.

These are original demo questions, not official course questions or a validated benchmark.
